    We estimate the subgroup's best-fit growth regime by searching 
    over an admissible class $\mathcal{H}$ of candidate CDFs on $[0,1]$.
    In this panel, $\mathcal{H}$ is the \emph{Beta regime family}
    \[
      \mathcal{H}_{\mathrm{Beta}}
      =\left\{\,H_{m,\kappa}(\cdot)\;:\;P\sim\mathrm{Beta}(\kappa m,\kappa(1-m)),\;0<m<1,\;\kappa>0\,\right\},
    \]
    
    \vspace{1pt}
    where $m=\mathbb{E}[P]$ controls the regime center (mean SGPc on the 0--1 scale) and
    $\kappa$ controls concentration (dispersion of latent SGPc).$^\mbox{2}$
    
    \vspace{4pt}
    For each candidate $H\in\mathcal{H}$, Panel~B provides the 
    induced \emph{predicted} current-grade marginal
    \[
      F_{H}(v)\;=\;\mathbb{E}_{U}\left[\,H\bigl(\Fzero(v\mid U)\bigr)\right].
    \]

    \vspace{1pt}
    The growth regime estimate for subgroup $S$ is defined by the minimum-distance problem
    \[
      \widehat{H}_S \;=\;
      \argmin_{H\in\mathcal{H}_{\mathrm{Beta}}}\;
      \Wone\bigl(F^{\mathrm{obs}}_{V,S},\,F_{H}\bigr),
    \]

    \vspace{1pt}
    where
    \[
      \Wone(F,G)=\int_0^1 \bigl|F(v)-G(v)\bigr|\,dv
    \]

    \vspace{1pt}
    is the Wasserstein-1 (earth mover's) distance between the observed and predicted current-grade marginal CDFs. 
    
    \vspace{4pt}
    The heat map displays $\log_{10}$ Wasserstein--1 distance over $(m,\log_{10}\kappa)$:
    lighter regions indicate candidate regimes whose induced $F_H$ more closely 
    matches $F^{\mathrm{obs}}_{V,S}$. The marked optimum corresponds to $\widehat{H}_S$ 
    (equivalently, $(\hat m,\hat\kappa)$). For reference, the uniform regime $H(p)=p$ 
    occurs at $m=0.5$ and $\kappa=2$ (i.e., $\mathrm{Beta}(1,1)$), indicated by the $U(0,1)$ marker.
