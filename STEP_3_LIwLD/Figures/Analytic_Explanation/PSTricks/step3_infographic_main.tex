\vspace{6pt}
\fnline{3}{Other regime families considered include the truncated exponential family (the maximum entropy probability distribution for a random variable restricted to a finite interval with a fixed mean) and the truncated uniform family.}

\vspace{4pt}
\fnline{4}{Here, $U$ denotes the subgroup's prior-grade reference quantile (pseudo-observation), and $C_0$ is the canonical baseline copula selected from STEP 1 by content area and year span. The fitted growth regime $\widehat{H}_S$ (distribution of latent $P=\SGPc/100$) and $C_0$ define a forward map
\[
(U,\;C_0,\;\widehat{H}_S)\ \longmapsto\ F_{\widehat{H}_S}(v)=\mathbb{E}_U\!\left[\widehat{H}_S\!\left(F_0(v\mid U)\right)\right],
\]
with corresponding induced density $f_{\widehat{H}_S}(v)$. The latent SGPc distribution itself is given by $\widehat{H}_S$ (density $\widehat{h}_S(p)=d\widehat{H}_S/dp$), which is estimated by matching $F_{\widehat{H}_S}$ to the observed current marginal.}

\vspace{4pt}
\fnline{5}{We assume $P\perp U$. That is, $p$ is drawn independently of starting point within the subgroup; meaning there is no correlation between the starting point $u$ and the growth regime. This assumption is investigated more deeply in the full paper.}

\vspace{4pt}
\fnline{6}{The Beta family, $\mathrm{Beta}(\alpha, \beta)$, is reparameterized via $\alpha = \kappa m$ and $\beta = \kappa(1-m)$, so that $m = \mathbb{E}[P] = \alpha / (\alpha + \beta)$ (the regime center, mean \SGPc~on the 0--1 scale) and $\kappa = \alpha + \beta$ (the concentration parameter).}
