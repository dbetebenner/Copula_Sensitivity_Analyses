    Let $C_0$ be a baseline copula (empirical or parametric) calibrated from 
    longitudinal data, inducing a transition kernel
    $F_0(v\mid u)=\Pr(V\le v\mid U=u)=\partial C_0(u,v)/\partial u$.

    \vspace{4pt}
    We posit a \emph{growth regime} $\symbf{H_\theta}$ for the latent conditional 
    percentile $P\in(0,1)$ (SGPc scale), with the key assumption $P\perp U$ within 
    the subgroup.

    \vspace{4pt}
    Then the implied current-grade marginal is computed deterministically by

    \[
    F_\theta(v)=\mathbb{E}\thinspace\big[H_\theta\thinspace\big(F_0(v\mid U)\big)\big]
    \approx \frac{\sum_i w_i\,H_\theta(F_0(v\mid u_i))}{\sum_i w_i}.
    \]

    \vspace{4pt}
    Uniform growth corresponds to $\symbf{H(p)=p}$. Departures from uniformity 
    tilt mass within the baseline kernel and change the implied $\symbf{V}$ marginal.
