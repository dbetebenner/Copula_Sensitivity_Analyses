    \textbf{\textcolor{black}{B.\enspace The analytic identity}}

    \vspace{6pt}
    Given a candidate growth regime~$\Htheta$ and the baseline
    transition kernel~$\Fzero(v\!\mid\!u)$ from the fitted copula, the
    \textbf{analytic identity}
    \[
      \Ftheta(v)
        \;=\; \mathbb{E}_U\!\bigl[\,\Htheta\!\bigl(\Fzero(v\!\mid\!U)\bigr)\bigr]
        \;=\; \frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^{n}\Htheta\!\bigl(\Fzero(v\!\mid\!u_i)\bigr)
    \]
    predicts the current-grade CDF \emph{exactly}---no Monte Carlo
    simulation is needed. The uniform regime $H(p)=p$ (grey dashed)
    systematically misses the observed CDF, while the inferred
    $H_{\thetahat}$ (teal dashed) closes the gap.
