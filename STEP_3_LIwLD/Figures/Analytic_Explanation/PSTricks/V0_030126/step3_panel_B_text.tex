    Let $C_0$ be a baseline copula (empirical or parametric) calibrated from longitudinal data.
    $C_0$ provides a \emph{dependence template} for how students move 
    from $U$ to $V$ and induces the conditional CDF (transition kernel)
    \[
      \Fzero(v\mid u)\;=\;\Pr(V\le v\mid U=u)\;=\;\frac{\partial}{\partial u}C_0(u,v).
    \]

    \vspace{1pt}
    Its inverse in $v$, the conditional quantile function/kernel, is
    \[
      Q_0(p\mid u)=\Fzero^{-1}(p\mid u).
    \]

    \vspace{1pt}
    Intuitively, for each starting quantile $u$, the kernel $\Fzero(\cdot\mid u)$ 
    describes the distribution of possible ending quantiles $V$ under the baseline 
    dependence structure $C_0$.

    \vspace{4pt}
    Let $P\in[0,1]$ be a random variable (i.e., a latent SGPc on the $0$--$1$ scale) 
    and $p$ denote a realization in $[0,1]$. A \emph{growth regime}, $H$, is the 
    distribution of $P$ represented by its CDF:
    \[
      H(p)\;:=\;\Pr(P\le p),\qquad p\in[0,1].
    \]

    \vspace{1pt}
    Given $U=u$ and a draw $P=p$, the model generates an outcome percentile by$^\mbox{1}$
    \[
      V \;=\; Q_0(P\mid U=u).
    \]

    \vspace{1pt}
    A growth regime governs how the subgroup $S$ ``occupies'' the baseline 
    kernel $\Fzero(\cdot\mid u)$. The baseline growth regime
    is Uniform$(0,1)$ and its CDF is the identity on $[0,1]$. That is, 
    \[
      P \sim \mbox{Uniform}(0,1) \quad \Longleftrightarrow \quad H(p)=p.
    \]

    \vspace{1pt}
    Departures from $H(p)=p$ tilt mass toward lower or higher conditional 
    percentiles relative to the uniform baseline regime. Although we 
    do not observe pairs $(u_i,v_i)$ in TIMSS- or NAEP-style settings, 
    the model provides a \emph{forward map} from the prior to current margin.

    \vspace{4pt}
    Thus, for any fixed $u$,
    \begin{eqnarray*}
      \Pr(V\le v\mid U=u)
      &=& \Pr\bigl(Q_0(P\mid u)\le v\bigr) \\
      &=& \Pr\bigl(P\le \Fzero(v\mid u)\bigr) \\
      &=& H\bigl(\Fzero(v\mid u)\bigr).
    \end{eqnarray*}

    Therefore, by the law of total probability, the subgroup's predicted current-grade marginal CDF is
    \begin{eqnarray*}
      \FH(v)
      &=& \Pr(V\le v) \\
      &=& \mathbb{E}_{U}\left[\,H\bigl(\Fzero(v\mid U)\bigr)\right] \\
      &=& \int_0^1 H\bigl(\Fzero(v\mid u)\bigr)\,dF_U(u) \\
      &\approx& \frac{\sum_{i=1}^{n} w_i\,H\bigl(\Fzero(v\mid u_i)\bigr)}{\sum_{i=1}^{n} w_i},
    \end{eqnarray*}

    \vspace{1pt}
    where $\{u_i,w_i\}$ are the observed prior pseudo-observations and 
    survey weights for the subgroup (i.e., a weighted empirical approximation to $F_U$).
