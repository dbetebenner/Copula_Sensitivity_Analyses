    We estimate $\theta$ by matching the predicted marginal 
    $F_\theta$ to the observed $F_{\mathrm{obs}}$ using a distributional 
    distance, here the Wasserstein-1 (earth mover) metric on $v\in[0,1]$.

    \vspace{4pt}
    For a Beta regime parameterization $P\sim\mathrm{Beta}(\kappa m,\kappa(1-m))$ (mean $m$ and 
    concentration $\kappa$), we evaluate the distance on a coarse grid over $(m,\kappa)$ and refine 
    locally (e.g., L-BFGS-B).

    \vspace{4pt}
    The heatmap shows $\log_{10}$ distance: lighter regions indicate regime 
    parameters that reproduce the observed current-grade distribution more closely. 
    The marked point is the minimizer $\hat\theta$.
