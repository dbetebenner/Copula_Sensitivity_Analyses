    \textbf{\textcolor{black}{C.\enspace Minimising the distance}}

    \vspace{6pt}
    We search over candidate parameter vectors
    $\theta = (\mathrm{mean},\;\kappa)$ of the Beta growth regime,
    computing the Wasserstein-1 distance
    \[
      \Wone\!\bigl(\Fobs,\;\Ftheta\bigr)
    \]
    at each grid point.  The heatmap shows $\log_{10}$ distance:
    dark regions are far from the observed CDF; light regions are close.

    \vspace{4pt}
    A two-stage procedure---coarse grid scan followed by
    L-BFGS-B refinement---locates $\thetahat$, the parameter vector
    that minimises the distance between the predicted and observed
    marginal CDFs of~$V$.
