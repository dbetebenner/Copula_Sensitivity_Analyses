    The fitted regime $H_{\hat\theta}$ (teal) is a distribution of latent 
    conditional percentiles $P$ (SGPc scale), i.e., how the subgroup allocates 
    conditional ranks \emph{within} the baseline dependence kernel $F_0(\cdot\mid u)$.

    \vspace{4pt}
    A regime centred below $50$ indicates systematically lower conditional ranks 
    relative to the reference population, while the concentration $\kappa$ quantifies 
    dispersion (a ``common-cause'' spread in percentile allocation).

    \vspace{4pt}
    In this synthetic validation, the recovered regime closely matches the generating 
    regime and yields an implied $V$ distribution that overlaps the observed current-grade marginal.
