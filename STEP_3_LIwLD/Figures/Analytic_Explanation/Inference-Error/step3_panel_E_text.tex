    The final panel answers the operational question: \emph{what total
    uncertainty attaches to a reported subgroup mean \SGPc, and how stable
    are resulting classifications?}

    \vspace{4pt}
    For each of five representative subgroups (at $N = 500$,
    independent design), Panel~E displays nested uncertainty layers:

    \vspace{2pt}
    \hspace{5pt}$\bullet$\;\textcolor{truthAmber}{$\bullet$} \textbf{Truth}
    (amber dot): $\thetaTrue$ from linked data.

    \vspace{1pt}
    \hspace{5pt}$\bullet$\;$\bullet$ \textbf{Bridge estimate} (black dot):
    $\thetaBridge$. The \textcolor{zissouRed}{arrow} between truth and
    estimate is the bridge bias (accuracy cost).

    \vspace{1pt}
    \hspace{5pt}$\bullet$\;\textcolor{samplingBlue}{$\blacksquare$}
    \textbf{Sampling band}: 95\% CI from repeated sampling
    ($\pm\,\samplingHWindep$ SGPc).

    \vspace{1pt}
    \hspace{5pt}$\bullet$\;\textcolor{stressOrange}{$\square$}
    \textbf{Stress-test band}: additional sensitivity to copula family choice,
    $P \perp U$ relaxation, and classification perturbation.

    \vspace{4pt}
    The total uncertainty envelope combines:
    \[
      \underbrace{\text{bridge bias}}_{\text{Panel B}}
      \;+\;
      \underbrace{\text{sampling precision}}_{\text{Panel D}}
      \;+\;
      \underbrace{\text{model sensitivity}}_{\text{stress tests}}.
    \]

    \vspace{4pt}
    Action thresholds and category labels are meaningful only within this
    combined envelope. A subgroup classified as ``low growth'' at
    $\thetaHat = 35$ is robust if the full band stays below 40; it is
    fragile if the band spans 30--45.$^{\mbox{\footnotesize{5}}}$
