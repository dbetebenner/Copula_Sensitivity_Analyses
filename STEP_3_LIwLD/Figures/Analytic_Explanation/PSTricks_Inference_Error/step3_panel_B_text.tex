    Panel~B operationalizes \emph{bridge accuracy} across $\nConditions$
    validation conditions. For each condition $r$:

    \vspace{2pt}
    \hspace{5pt}1.~Compute the truth $\thetaTrue$ from linked data.

    \vspace{1pt}
    \hspace{5pt}2.~Erase the pairings; retain only the unlinked marginals
                  $F_U$ and $F_V$.

    \vspace{1pt}
    \hspace{5pt}3.~Re-run the copula bridge (STEP~3) to obtain
                  $\thetaBridge$.

    \vspace{1pt}
    \hspace{5pt}4.~Record the bridge error:

    \[
      \text{Bridge error}_r \;=\; \thetaBridge - \thetaTrue.
    \]

    \vspace{4pt}
    The scatter plot shows \emph{truth vs.\ bridge estimate} for each
    condition. Points near the identity line indicate high accuracy.
    The amber band marks $\pm 2$ SGPc points from perfect agreement.

    \vspace{4pt}
    Accuracy is summarized by center displacement, not by interval width.
    The relevant metrics are mean absolute error (MAE~$= \maeBridge$),
    mean signed error ($\meanBridgeError$), and RMSE ($\rmseBridge$),
    all on the 0--100 SGPc scale.

    \vspace{4pt}
    This validation answers: \emph{how far does the unlinked bridge move us
    from the truth that linked data would provide?}$^{\mbox{\footnotesize{2}}}$
