## Analysis Plan

### Primary Research Question

**Did the COVID-19 pandemic (2020 school closures) disrupt the copula dependency structure in longitudinal educational assessments?**

### Comparison Strategy

For each of the 10 pandemic pairs, we have a matched baseline pair:

- **Same grade pairs** (e.g., G3→G5 vs. G3→G5)
- **Same time span** (2-year or 3-year)
- **Same content area** (MATHEMATICS or READING)
- **Different time period** (pandemic vs. pre-pandemic)

### Key Parameters to Compare

1. **Kendall's τ** (overall dependence)
   - Expected range: 0.60-0.75
   - Hypothesis: May decrease if pandemic weakened dependence

2. **Degrees of Freedom (ν)** for t-copula
   - Expected range: 5-15
   - Hypothesis: May decrease (heavier tails) if pandemic increased extreme outcomes

3. **Tail Dependence (λ)**
   - Expected range: 0.10-0.25
   - Hypothesis: May increase if extreme students became more extreme

### Expected Outputs

After running STEP 1, each condition will produce:

- **Copula fits:** All 6 families (gaussian, t, clayton, gumbel, frank, comonotonic)
- **Best family:** AIC-based selection (expected: t-copula)
- **Parameters:** ρ, τ, df, tail dependence
- **Visualizations:** Contour plots (CDF, PDF, comparison, uncertainty)
- **SGPc:** Copula-based growth percentiles (if enabled)

After running pandemic analysis script:

- **Parameter comparison table:** Pandemic vs. baseline for all 10 pairs
- **Change metrics:** Δτ, Δdf, Δtail_dep
- **Visualizations:** Change plots, scatter plots
- **Summary report:** Interpretation and findings
